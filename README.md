# The World Today — Wednesday 25 November 2026

A newspaper front page summing up the world news of Wednesday 25 November 2026, drawn entirely by code: a hand-built 5x7 bitmap font, rectangles, lines and circles — no images, no network, no dependencies. The program writes a 1024×1024 PNG to `/output/entry.png`.

## The five stories

1. **Space — China and Pakistan.** China's Shenzhou-24 launched at 8:18 a.m. Beijing time from Jiuquan on a Long March 2F, carrying Tang Hongbo, Wu Yi and Pakistan's Muhammad Zeeshan Ali — the first Pakistani in orbit and the first foreign astronaut to visit the Tiangong station.
2. **The Pacific — Security.** Australia and Solomon Islands signed a security pact in Canberra, making Australia the islands' preferred defence partner, with a bigger role in policing.
3. **Markets — Asia.** In early trading, Korea's KOSPI fell 4.5%, Japan's Nikkei fell 2.5% and Taiwan fell more than 3% as an AI-led sell-off spread from Wall Street.
4. **Sydney — City.** Busy streets in Sydney's central business district were closed after a major gas leak. Elizabeth St, Market St and King St were shut as HAZMAT fire crews moved in. No casualties were mentioned.
5. **India — Society.** India is to launch the fifth Nayi Chetna gender campaign, on the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women. It runs to 23 December, focused on women's economic agency, safety and shared care work.

Sources: Reuters (Northland News Radio, SRN News), Traders Union, Daily Fox News, YourStory. All events of Wednesday 25 November 2026.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This writes `out/entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB). Running it twice produces byte-identical output.
